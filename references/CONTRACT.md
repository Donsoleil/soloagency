# How these skills are supposed to behave

Four rules. Every skill in this pack follows them, and a skill that does not
is a bug worth reporting.

## 1. It tells you what it is based on

Every skill ends by saying what its answer rests on: a number it read, a file
it wrote, a page that loads, a count from your own accounts. When it could not
get that, it says `NOT VERIFIED` and tells you what would settle it.

The point is that you can tell the difference between an answer built on your
actual numbers and an answer built on a sensible-sounding assumption. Both feel
the same to read. Only one is safe to act on.

## 2. It looks before it asks

If your payment account knows what you charge, it reads it rather than asking
you to remember. Nobody recalls their own discounts accurately, and a plan
built on a misremembered price is wrong from the first line.

It also reads your notebook at `~/.soloagency/memory.md` before asking anything
you have already answered.

## 3. It says when a choice is one-way

Some decisions you can try and reverse by Friday. Ship the page, watch what
happens, change it back.

Others you cannot. Raising the price on customers who already signed up.
Anything with a signature. Building your setup around a regulation. Those get
flagged as one-way before the recommendation, not after, so you can slow down
where slowing down is worth it.

## 4. It tells you when the answer is somewhere else

These skills cover the business side. When your question turns out to be about
code, or interface design, or something that needs filing with an actual
authority, the skill says so and points you at the right kind of help.

A skill that pretends to cover everything covers nothing, and you would have no
way to tell which answers to trust.

## Writing things down

When a decision gets made, it goes in your notebook with the reasoning and a
blank line for what happened later. Fill that line in eventually, including
when it went badly. A record of only your good decisions will make you
confident about the wrong things.

## Not advice

Nothing in this pack is legal, tax, or financial advice. It is preparation for
a conversation with someone licensed to give it.
